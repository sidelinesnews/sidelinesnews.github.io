# Sidelines: founding statement

*The dot outside the bubble.*

Written 2 November 2026. This file is frozen: it is never edited. Its SHA-256 fingerprint is stamped into the Bitcoin ledger with OpenTimestamps (proof: `FOUNDING.md.ots`), so anyone can check that it existed, unchanged, from this date.

## What Sidelines is

An open-source news aggregator for the stories that never make it to the main stage. It reads a wide pool of independent, regional, local, science and across-the-spectrum outlets, pushes down what the big mainstream outlets are already running, and stops any single source from dominating.

## The promise

Sidelines will never:

- run ads
- sell or rent placement on the page
- track readers
- sell or share data about anyone
- let money buy any say over what appears on the page

The ranking rules are public, and any change to them happens in the open.

Sidelines takes no money for itself. Support goes to the journalists and to free-press organizations.

## Legacy

If Sidelines grows beyond a one-person project, or if its founder dies or can no longer care for it, everything that is the founder's to give (the code, the repository, the Sidelines name, and any funds) is intended for the Freedom of the Press Foundation, or, if they decline, another independent, non-commercial journalism or press-freedom organization that will keep this promise.

The code belongs to everyone under the AGPL-3.0, so the idea can't be owned or buried.
